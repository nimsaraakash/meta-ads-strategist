# Meta platform changes (as known October 2026)

**Caveat:** most items come from agency and vendor blogs, not Meta documentation. Items marked *(single source)* had only one source. Before a recommendation depends on any of these, run a quick web search to confirm it still holds, and add newer changes to this file.

| When | Change | Practical effect |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 2024 → Oct 2025 | **Andromeda** ad retrieval rolled out globally. There is no separate "Oct 2026 Andromeda update"; the label refers to the rollout. | Creative does the targeting; distinct concepts matter, look-alike ads are grouped. |
| Early 2026 | Detailed targeting became a suggestion only (with Advantage+ audience). | Interests steer, they don't restrict. |
| Early 2026 | Click-through attribution counts **link clicks only**; new engage-through category (likes, shares, saves, 5 s+ video views). | Boosted posts without links produce little click-through. |
| 2026 rolling | "Views / viewers" replacing reach and impressions as headline metrics. | Update reporting templates. |
| Jan 2026 | Facebook Reels ranking via interest survey model; Reels reach reportedly up, standard post reach down (industry figures). | Organic: favour Reels. |
| 27 Jul 2026 | Advantage+ creative can rewrite text on images (up to 8 headline variants). | Set brand restricted words. |
| 29 Jul 2026 | LLM-based generative recommender in ad ranking. | More weight on creative signal. |
| 10 Aug 2026 | Exclusion-only custom audiences. | Suppress buyers/followers cleanly. |
| 17 Aug 2026 | Holiday Insights Center: Advantage+ sales with creator content live by mid-October. | Q4 timing. |
| 21 Aug 2026 | Ad-set placement exclusions being removed; value rules (bid a placement down up to 90%) and account-level controls remain. *(single source)* | Objective choice and account-level controls are the lever. |
| 26 Aug 2026 | Creative Diversity score (Low/Medium/High) per ad set. *(single source)* | One-ad ad sets score Low. |
| 15 Sep 2026 | Creator Marketing Hub (creator marketplace + partnership ads). Meta One subscription tiers gate some organic tools. | Creator content route. |
| 21 Sep 2026 | Advertising Settings switch lets Meta show some people an **AI-generated landing page** instead of your site. | Usually turn off. |
| 22 Sep 2026 | Threads ads no longer need an Instagram account. | Low priority. |
| 29 Sep 2026 | Instagram Live video ads generally available. | Event/showroom live tests. |
| 6–11 Oct 2026 | AI assistant in Ads Manager that builds/edits campaigns; Customer Lifecycle Strategy toggle (spend only on new customers). | Review anything it changes. |
| Oct 2026 | Marketing API v24 sunset; Embedded Signup v2/v3 deprecated; Commerce Order Management API sunset (27 Oct). | Only if third-party tools are connected. |
| 30 Oct 2026 | New Commercial / Self-serve Ad / Generative AI terms; Meta may run delivery and creative tests without notice. | Someone with authority reviews before accepting. |

## Benchmarks (directional)

- Facebook page organic engagement: median ~0.15% of followers; image ~0.11%, Reels ~0.23%; organic reach ~1.65% of followers.
- Published small-budget case: 5.15x ROAS ($9K from $1.8K) with one campaign per offer, broad targeting, 2 statics + 2 videos, losers swapped every 1–2 weeks (category undisclosed).
