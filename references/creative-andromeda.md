# Creative strategy under Andromeda

## How delivery works now

- **Andromeda** is Meta's ad retrieval system (announced Dec 2024, global by Oct 2025). Delivery starts from the creative; the ad itself tells Meta who to show it to.
- Ads that look alike are grouped and treated as **one** concept. Changing a headline, background colour, crop or font does not make a new ad.
- A new concept = a different **argument** (why buy) and a different **visual world** (setting, people, format).
- Guidance seen in 2026: 8–15 genuinely different concepts per campaign at scale; 4–6 at small budgets.
- Consolidation test widely cited: one ad set with 25 varied creatives beat five ad sets of five (~17% more conversions, ~16% lower cost). Attribution of the test varies — treat as indicative.
- A per-ad-set **Creative Diversity score** (Low/Medium/High) was reported from Aug 2026 (single source). One-ad ad sets score Low.

## Concept angles to cover

Pick 4–8 that differ on both message and visual:

| Angle | Example execution |
|---|---|
| Product hero | Real product photo, what you receive (box, pieces, set) |
| Product in context / usage | Table laid, gift being opened, hands using it |
| Maker / founder / behind the scenes | 10–15 s phone video in the real workshop or kitchen |
| Unboxing / delivery | Parcel arriving and opened; for gifts, the recipient's reaction |
| Offer / price | Price or tiers on the image; "ships free over X"; instalments |
| Gift / occasion | Dated: "order by 5 Dec for Christmas" |
| Social proof | Real customer quote, review stars, creator content |
| Personalisation | Build-your-own, custom message |
| How it works | 3 steps (order → entered → draw date) |
| Language / community version | Native-language copy for that audience |
| Carousel with prices | One product per card, last card = offer |

## Format rules

- Feed: **4:5 (1080×1350)**. Stories/Reels: **9:16 (1080×1920)**. 2:3 posters get cropped top and bottom.
- Static images served into Reels perform poorly on reach-optimised campaigns; give Reels a vertical video.
- For food and physical goods a strong still can match video and is often cheaper per sale; test both.
- Real photography beats AI renders when the product must match what arrives (complaints/refunds risk).
- First line of copy carries the hook; for cross-border/diaspora name the route ("From [city abroad] to [family member]'s door").

## Copy patterns

- Winning-ad pattern worth reusing: short three-beat tagline → one sentence on what it is → one "for those who…" line.
- Promotions: state threshold, prize/discount, dates, number of winners, "T&C apply" in the first lines.
- Local offers: addresses and opening hours in copy; button matches the ad set (Call now / Send message / Shop now).
- Trust line on cross-border/gift ads: made where, since when, delivered to the door, breakage/replacement policy.
- Avoid interchangeable luxury adjectives ("refined", "timeless", "thoughtfully crafted") and trailing keyword strings.
- Prices in ads: test, don't assume. Some winning ads carry none; promo ads usually need them.

## Advantage+ creative enhancements

- Text rewrites, image touch-ups, video touch-ups, music, catalogue carousel add-ons are often on by default. Decide deliberately; set brand restricted words.

## Rotation

- Keep 4–6 live at small budgets; hold the rest as replacements.
- Rotate when an ad hits the kill rule (see measurement-and-math.md), not on a calendar.
- Keep the proven winner live — it holds social proof (reactions/comments) and history.
- Don't duplicate ad sets to force spend onto ads Meta ignores; replace the creative instead.

## What to ask the client for

- Phone footage is enough: maker video, unboxing, real delivery/reaction.
- Real product photos of what ships; 4:5 and 9:16 exports of any posters.
- A real customer quote with permission.
- Seasonal versions with an order-by date.
