# Measurement and the arithmetic

## Unit economics come first

- **Break-even ROAS** = 1 / gross margin (after COGS, shipping, payment fees). 40% margin → 2.5x.
- **Max CPA** = AOV × margin. If the best ad set's CPA is above what the buyer spends, no targeting tweak fixes it; raise order value or conversion rate.
- Lever check, in order of usual impact at small scale:
  1. **AOV** — bundles/kits, free-shipping threshold just above the typical order, gift sets. Published cases: single unit → kit took AOV $49 → $129 while CPA rose ~$15; collection-style ads lifted ROAS 62% almost entirely via +35% AOV.
  2. **Conversion rate** — zero-cost site fixes (see audit checklist §8).
  3. **CPA** — creative variety, consolidation, right optimisation event.
- Worked example: CPA $46, AOV $38 → 0.8x. Same CPA with an $84 bundle → ~1.8x.

## What to judge on

- Weekly **store revenue ÷ ad spend** (blended MER) alongside Meta ROAS. Meta usually under-reports vs the store's referrer data; occasionally over-reports if pixel+CAPI double-count.
- Offline businesses: calls placed, calls connected ≥20 s, conversations started, then ask the business to count qualifying bills/visits for the period vs a baseline period and ask customers how they heard.
- Prize/threshold promos: the client must report qualifying orders from the back end; Ads Manager can't see order value tiers.

## Sample sizes

- Under ~30 conversions, rates are directional. Say so.
- Don't restrict audience/placement on a handful of conversions — use creative to steer instead.
- An ad set needs ~50 optimisation events/week to exit learning. At $10–50/day it will stay "learning limited"; that is normal.

## Budget rules of thumb

- Small budget (<$30/day): one campaign, one broad ad set, 4–6 live ads.
- Split ad sets only when each can afford meaningful results (rough line: $100+/month per ad set for a test, more for sales).
- Fixed ad-set budgets (not CBO) when you must stop the cheapest market/result type swallowing spend.
- Lifetime budgets suit dated promotions; Meta weights the best days.
- Retargeting pool sizing: about **$5–10 per 1,000 people in the audience per week**. A pool under ~1,000 supports ~$1/day — usually not worth a separate ad set; Advantage+ already reaches warm users.

## Kill / rotate rules (adjust to price point)

- Ad spent ~1× target CPA (or a fixed amount like $30 at low AOV) with **no add-to-cart** → pause and rotate the next concept.
- Market/ad set with ~$100 spent and no order → new creative; second failure → move budget to the best market.
- Don't edit an ad set for 7–14 days after a significant change unless broken.
- Watch reach and frequency in the week after adding audience suggestions; if reach collapses, remove them.

## Estimating results

Always give ranges and their basis:
- Results = budget ÷ cost-per-result range from the account's own comparable campaigns (same objective, market, price point).
- If benchmarks come from cheaper products, say expected results sit at the low end or below.
- Chain rates explicitly: e.g. ATC → purchase rate from the account, enquiry → order rate from past campaigns.
- Untested markets: state the assumed cost range and that it is an assumption.
- Label targets as targets, not forecasts.

## Reporting cadence

- Weekly: spend, results, cost per result, store revenue / spend, frequency, top and bottom ads.
- Monthly / per campaign: one-page client summary — results, order value, best creative, next tests.
- Keep a campaign log (dates, budget, objective, results) so future plans have precedents.
