# Campaign playbooks

Each playbook: when to use, structure, audience, creative, measurement, pitfalls. Adapt numbers to the account's own history.

---

## A. Small-budget e-commerce (≈$10–50/day)

**Use when:** online store, low monthly spend, proving ads can pay.

- **Campaign:** one Sales campaign, website, optimise for Purchase (or Add-to-cart if purchases are very rare). Keep the campaign/ad set that holds purchase history and the winning ad's social proof.
- **Ad set:** one, broad (country, 18+, Advantage+ audience and placements on). Custom audiences only as *suggestions*.
- **Ads:** 4–6 live, distinct concepts; keep the winner; rotate on the kill rule.
- **No** separate retargeting ad set while the warm pool is under a few thousand people; **no** catalogue ads at this budget.
- **Fix the economics alongside:** bundle/kit, free-shipping threshold, abandoned-checkout email, placeholder pages.
- **Measure:** weekly store revenue ÷ spend; don't edit for 14 days after a restructure.
- **Pitfalls:** splitting budget into several ad sets; restricting age/placement on a handful of sales; sending all traffic to one low-price product page.

## B. Dated promotion with a spend threshold or prize draw

**Use when:** "spend X, get Y / win Z" with fixed dates.

- **Budget:** enough for delivery to settle — roughly 35+ optimisation events a week. Precedents from the account's past promos of similar length are the best guide.
- **Campaign:** one Sales campaign, website, optimised for add-to-cart (usually the only event with volume), lifetime budget across the exact offer dates (convert to account timezone).
- **Ad sets:** prospecting (larger share) + warm audiences targeted directly (visitors, engagers, customer list) if the budget is ≥ ~$100.
- **No reach campaign alongside** — reach next to promos historically gives near-zero add-to-carts; organic posts carry the "attention" brief.
- **Creative:** hero static naming the prize/discount; carousel of qualifying products with prices; native-language version; 10–15 s video; "how it works" in three steps; "last week"/"last 3 days" swaps.
- **Settle before artwork:** do discounted items qualify; exact start/end; named prize (named hotel/resort beats "a stay"); terms page (qualifying value, single vs cumulative order, draw method/date, validity, instalment orders, returns, "not sponsored by Meta"); landing page listing qualifying items; stock/pre-order; one contact number; customer list for upload.
- **Conflicts:** check overlapping promotions (online vs in-store) that pull the same buyer in opposite directions.
- **Expect:** qualifying orders often can't be estimated from Meta; give add-to-cart ranges and say one to a few qualifying orders may be a good result.

## C. Local store / showroom promotion

**Use when:** goal is visits/enquiries and Meta can't count store visits.

- **Measurable proxies:** calls placed, calls connected ≥20 s, conversations.
- **Campaign:** Engagement (or Leads) with two ad sets on fixed budgets — **Calls** and **Messages** — so the cheaper result doesn't take all spend.
- **Audience:** radius/districts within ~1 hour of a store; Advantage+ audience **off** (local limits must hold); age from the account's call/message breakdowns (callers often skew older; messengers younger); interests as proxies for the buyer types in the offer (households/weddings, hospitality buyers, corporate gifting).
- **Schedule:** call ads only during opening hours. One answered phone number.
- **Placements:** watch Reels connect rate — Reels calls often connect less (accidental taps).
- **Creative:** offer static that states the tiers/discount and store names; product carousel with before/after prices; one ad per buyer type; native language; store walk-through video.
- **Measure:** ask stores for qualifying bill counts vs the prior equal period and "how did you hear".

## D. Leads / messages / WhatsApp

**Use when:** high-consideration product, buyers want to talk, or the store can't sell to that market yet.

- Engagement campaign optimised for conversations; destinations Messenger, Instagram Direct, WhatsApp (needs a number connected to the page).
- Someone must answer, in the buyer's timezone. Log every enquiry: date, country, product, value, outcome.
- Graduate to a website Sales ad set once the store can fulfil.

## D2. Instant-form lead generation (schools, courses, services)

**Use when:** the sale happens offline after a callback or visit (admissions, enrolments, consultations).

- **Campaign:** Leads, instant form. "More volume" forms are cheap but low intent; "higher intent" adds a review step. Add 1–2 qualifying questions (e.g. grade/year, branch, intake).
- **Judge on the back end:** agree a simple sheet or CRM with the client (lead date, branch, contacted, visited, enrolled) before scaling. Report cost per visit/enrolment, not cost per lead.
- **Structure:** one campaign per intake. Few ad sets; handle branches/locations with targeting or copy rather than many $5 ad sets.
- **Placements:** check Audience Network's cost per lead. It is often several times Facebook feed.
- **Pair with call ads** in the final 1–2 weeks of an intake. Report calls connected 20 s+, not calls placed (typically ~20–30% connect).
- **Speed to lead:** leads contacted within hours convert far better. Confirm who calls them and how fast.

## E. Diaspora / cross-border gifting

**Use when:** selling to people abroad who send gifts home or buy a piece of home.

- **Audience:** behaviour "Lived in [country]" (expats), age 30+ with no upper cap unless data says otherwise; Advantage+ audience **off** (otherwise it expands far beyond expats); whole country per market.
- **Structure:** one always-on campaign, one ad set per market group on fixed budgets; reallocate after ~2 months by cost per order.
- **Idea:** find the angle competitors don't own (e.g. a durable keepsake vs flowers or cakes that are gone by evening).
- **Creative pillars:** reaction film of the recipient; "a piece of home for your table"; "the occasion you can't attend"; "coming home?". First line names the route. Trust line on every ad. Language versions per community.
- **Calendar:** monthly themes tied to home-country occasions (use home-country dates — the recipient is there); order-by dates.
- **From month 3:** carve ~20% for retargeting video viewers/messagers in the two weeks before each big occasion.
- **Research:** Ad Library search competitors by keyword per country; check review sites for their weak points (substitutions, damage, slow replies) and promise the opposite.

## F. Retargeting

- Advantage+ campaigns already reach warm users; a separate warm ad set often just competes with them.
- Run one only when the pool supports it ($5–10 per 1,000 people per week) — typically for a short pre-occasion push.
- If separate: Advantage+ audience **off**, exclude purchasers (exclusion-only audiences exist since Aug 2026).
- Build audiences early anyway — they back-fill and cost nothing: website visitors 180 d, add-to-cart 180 d, purchasers 365 d, FB/IG engagers 365 d, video viewers 50%+, customer list.
- Lookalikes need 100+ source people from one country; broad often does the same job.

## G. Awareness (only when it is genuinely the brief)

- Cap it at a fixed share of budget and still give it a measurable secondary action.
- Never ThruPlay with Advantage+ placements unchecked — it flows to Audience Network rewarded video.
- Exclude cheap-CPM regions that can't buy; set frequency caps and watch them.
- Prefer organic posts + incidental impressions from conversion campaigns.

## Holiday / seasonal planning

- Meta's Q4 guidance in 2026: sales campaigns with creator content live by **mid-October**; Black Friday late November.
- Seasonal creative with order-by dates; shipping constraints (e.g. heat for perishables, breakage for fragile goods) stated plainly.
